# Naturis

After civilization has vanished, nature reclaims the world. Mysterious new creatures spread throughout the wilderness, carving out a new habitat for themselves, while humanity is increasingly pushed aside.

Naturis adds a range of creatures and gives players new reasons to explore the world beyond scavenging and survival. Each creature has its own materials, behavior, and place in the ecosystem.

### What Naturis adds

- **New creatures:** A variety of new creatures, each with their own traits and abilities. Many of them have several states that can be triggered.
- **A living ecosystem:** Creatures can hunt, feed, defend themselves, lay eggs, and compete with other species.
- **Hunting and tracking:** Find monster habitats, investigate nests, and take on hunting missions.However, the goal is not a quest system. The player must decide for themselves what to hunt and why.
- **Research:** Collect samples and remains to learn more about creatures and unlock new crafting options.
- **Crafting:** Use monster materials alongside existing items and resources to make weapons, equipment, tools, and other useful gear.
- **New places to explore:** Discover abandoned hunting sites, overgrown structures, nests, and unusual natural formations.

### The idea behind Naturis

The goal is to make the creatures feel like part of the world rather than just another type of enemy. They should have their own habitats, interact with other wildlife, and make the wilderness more dangerous and interesting to explore.

The mod is intended to give the player an incentive to venture into the wilderness, rather than focusing solely on urban areas. Materials obtained from creatures can be crafted into new weapons and other items.

Rather than existing alongside the main game, naturis is intended to integrate seamlessly by utilizing existing CTLG areas, expanding upon them, and incorporating the resources already present.
